## Interpreting pilot_observations.csv
paired_case_id links modes for the same source_joiner_id. task_seconds measures operator activity, not worker readiness or end-to-end delivery. latest_start_date_visible, owner_approval_visible and expiry_supported are observer findings, not a complete security/access assessment. operator_assisted affects independence.

Only one desktop browser and a small convenient operator group were used. No full independent-client set, peak load, mobile/accessibility, token expiry, outage or leaver-session study exists. The supplier's claim of simpler onboarding cannot establish all-required-access readiness.

## Separate follow-up aggregates
Two ten-person synthetic cohorts were reviewed outside the June data. Baseline: 9 tickets closed before start, 7 core logins by day one, 6 manager-confirmed required-access outcomes and 34 service hours. Trial cohort: 10 closures, 8 core logins, 6 required-access confirmations and 43 hours.

The groups differ in remote/client mix and the trial has dedicated support. No exact start-time or representative productivity study exists. The prototype remained isolated. Neither the closure improvement nor unchanged confirmations proves causal benefit, failure or sustainable effort. No leaver outcome is measured in those cohort totals.
