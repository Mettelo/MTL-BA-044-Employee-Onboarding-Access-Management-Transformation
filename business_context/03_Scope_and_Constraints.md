# Scope and constraints

From approved hire through day-one readiness and initial access review; include mover and leaver dependencies affecting access controls. Include equipment, remote delivery, approvals, identity and audit evidence. Exclude payroll replacement and a full HR platform procurement.

HR system export is daily. Directory connector supports disabling accounts but application sessions may persist. No shared credentials or blanket access package is authorised. Employment details must remain within approved purposes and access groups.

No production access is provided. Staff can attend one 45-minute workshop each week. Finance needs one-off and recurring costs separately, with assumptions about effort and adoption. Any change to policy requires the named policy owner; funding approval alone does not approve a control exception.
