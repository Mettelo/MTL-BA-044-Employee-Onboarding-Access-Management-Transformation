# Existing JML procedure — fictional version 2

HR owns effective employment dates; the line manager owns role and client assignment. IT provisions approved technical access. Facilities manages passes. Leaver access should cease at the effective leaving time; verify directory and independently managed applications.
The older service-desk article says 'disable at 17:00 on leaving date'. It is not revised for a midday or immediate departure. HR may correct dates after export, but this procedure does not define how changes are acknowledged by every queue.
Manager submits planned joiners at least five working days ahead where possible. Late submission does not waive access approval.
