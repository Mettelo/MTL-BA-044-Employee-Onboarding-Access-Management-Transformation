# Existing access, JML and information-handling sources
**Mettelo BA-044 · Stage 3 · Fictional internal policy, not legislation**

## Source authority
HR owns approved employment facts and effective dates. Managers confirm business need. Application owners authorise relevant entitlements. IT provisions technical access. Security approves privileged/emergency exceptions. Facilities owns passes. No source assigns all future end-to-end accountability or supplies an approved role/access matrix.

## Access policy v3 — 1 April
Access requires appropriate business purpose and owner approval. Privileged access needs separate Security review and defined review/expiry conditions. Same-as-colleague is not enough evidence to grant all accumulated permissions. Shared credentials are prohibited.

A manager's urgency does not authorise their own privileged exception. Emergency access needs recorded appropriate approval, time limit and retrospective review. This is a fictional internal control, not a statutory threshold.

## JML procedure v2
HR effective dates are authoritative employment facts; managers own business assignment. Changes should reach relevant operational owners. The supplied procedure does not define a reliable acknowledgement or automatic reopening of a closed ticket.

Leaver access should cease at the effective leaving time and relevant independent applications/passes must be checked. An older service article says disable at 17:00 on leaving date, without treatment of noon/immediate departures. The source set does not resolve the conflict for the participant.

Mover changes can require removal and addition. A joiner portal that only adds permissions may leave an unresolved dependency. No complete mover population is supplied.

## Completion and evidence
The existing service desk accepts device dispatch/account creation as closure evidence. HR and managers want verified readiness; the terms differ. A directory event is not evidence that every client application, active session or pass is resolved. Blank confirmation must remain explicitly unverified.

Approvals should identify what need/entitlement was authorised and by whom. Email-only evidence may be incomplete or elsewhere; investigation is required before a conclusion. Record version and change meaning, not just a generic tick.

## Worker information
Share only relevant fields for the stated operational purpose. Home delivery information belongs in an approved restricted channel. Facilities does not need salary or medical data; application owners need relevant role/purpose, not full HR records.

The retention schedule is not supplied. Do not invent a duration or assume retain everything. Supplier access, export, deletion and incident arrangements need the appropriate review. Use synthetic records only in this training repository.

## Outstanding policy questions
Who acknowledges date corrections? Which application owner handles an unassigned client? What evidence demonstrates readiness by the defined start? How should independent revocation be verified? Which authority approves a genuine emergency and its expiry?

These are source-owner questions, not a completed gap analysis. Participants must trace and validate their own proposed rules, access/control needs, process exceptions, acceptance criteria and transition plan.
